Discover the Beauty of Goa

Goa is one of the most popular tourist destinations in India. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, Portuguese architecture, delicious seafood, churches, festivals and nightlife. Tourists from different parts of the world visit Goa to enjoy its natural beauty and peaceful atmosphere.

About Goa

Goa is located on the western coast of India. Panaji is its capital city. Goa is known for its rich culture, historical monuments, scenic landscapes and hospitality.

Famous Tourist Places in Goa

Baga Beach

Famous for water sports, beach shacks and nightlife.

Calangute Beach

Popular for its beautiful coastline and tourist activities.

Old Goa

Known for its historic churches and Portuguese heritage.
